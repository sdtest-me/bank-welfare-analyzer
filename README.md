# English

## Overview

Bank Welfare Analyzer is an experimental analytical system designed to evaluate how banking behavior aligns — or conflicts — with the socio-economic reality of the population it serves.

Unlike traditional banking ratings focused on profitability, liquidity, or shareholder return, this system models:

- behavioral mismatch,
- welfare extraction risk,
- institutional alignment,
- socio-economic pressure,
- long-term systemic sustainability.

The project combines:

- macroeconomic indicators,
- lending structure analysis,
- ESG interpretation,
- Spiral Dynamics modeling,
- mismatch detection,
- narrative-based risk interpretation.

The result is not a credit rating.

It is a behavioral-systemic diagnostic layer.

# 1. Core Concepts

## Welfare Alignment

The system estimates whether a bank:

- supports productive economic development,
- extracts value from vulnerable populations,
- creates structurally unstable lending dynamics.

The model analyzes:

- lending composition,
- income vs profit growth,
- poverty context,
- affordability pressure,
- ESG consistency,
- population-stage compatibility.

---

## Spiral Dynamics Modeling

The project uses concepts from Spiral Dynamics theory to estimate value-system distributions for:

- populations,
- institutions,
- banking behavior.

The system models interactions between:

- Beige survival pressure,
- Red extraction dynamics,
- Blue institutional discipline,
- Orange achievement systems,
- Green social legitimacy,
- Yellow adaptive flexibility.

This is not psychological profiling.

It is a macro-behavioral interpretation layer built from economic indicators and institutional signals.

---

## Mismatch Analysis

The core engine calculates:

- structural mismatch,
- dominant-stage conflict,
- pressure asymmetry,
- ESG inconsistency,
- behavioral divergence.

Examples:

- Red bank vs Beige population
- Blue compliance masking extractive lending
- Green ESG narratives with high affordability stress
- Orange growth models disconnected from local reality

---

# 2. Impact Index

The system generates a unified:

# Impact Index (0–100)

The index combines:

- welfare conditions,
- behavioral mismatch,
- lending pressure,
- ESG alignment,
- institutional sustainability.

## General Interpretation

| Range | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 0–39 | Structural extraction risk |
| 40–59 | Transitional instability |
| 60–79 | Moderate alignment |
| 80–100 | Stable welfare alignment |

# 1. Ключевые концепции

## Welfare Alignment

Система оценивает:

- поддерживает ли банк продуктивное развитие экономики,
- извлекает ли ценность из уязвимых групп населения,
- формирует ли структурно нестабильную модель кредитования.

Анализируются:

- структура кредитования,
- разрыв между ростом прибыли и доходов населения,
- контекст бедности,
- affordability pressure,
- ESG consistency,
- совместимость стадий населения и банка.

---

## Моделирование Spiral Dynamics

Проект использует идеи Spiral Dynamics для оценки распределения ценностных систем:

- населения,
- банков,
- институционального поведения.

Модель анализирует взаимодействия:

- Beige выживания,
- Red экстракции,
- Blue дисциплины,
- Orange достижения,
- Green социальной легитимности,
- Yellow адаптивности.

Это не психологическое профилирование.

Это макро-поведенческий интерпретационный слой на основе экономических и институциональных сигналов.

---

## Анализ mismatch

Ядро системы рассчитывает:

- структурный mismatch,
- конфликт доминирующих стадий,
- pressure asymmetry,
- ESG inconsistency,
- поведенческую дивергенцию.

Примеры:

- Red банк vs Beige население
- Blue compliance как маска экстрактивности
- Green ESG-риторика при высокой долговой нагрузке
- Orange growth disconnected from local reality
