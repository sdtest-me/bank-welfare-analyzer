# Bank Welfare Analyzer (BWA) Pilot Charter

## 1) What BWA is and is NOT

### BWA is
- A **deterministic, rules-based analytical system** for welfare alignment analysis of banks.
- A framework that applies **Spiral Dynamics vMeme stage distribution logic** to public macroeconomic and bank-level indicators.
- A decision-support instrument that produces transparent outputs: **Welfare Score (0–100), Mismatch Score, Spiral Gap Analysis, Impact Index, and data provenance indicators**.

### BWA is NOT
- Not an AI prediction system.
- Not a machine-learning model that infers hidden patterns from private or unverified data.
- Not a credit-rating replacement, supervisory mandate, or legal compliance substitute.
- Not a guarantee of financial, regulatory, or social outcomes.

## 2) Pilot purpose and scope

### Purpose
The pilot validates whether BWA outputs are:
1. Clear for executive and risk decision contexts.
2. Trusted due to deterministic logic and traceable evidence.
3. Useful for prioritizing strategy, risk dialogue, and welfare-oriented action planning.

### Scope
Pilot scope includes:
- Preset-based analysis for participating banks.
- Review of Welfare Score, Mismatch Score, Spiral Gap Analysis, and Impact Index.
- Examination of data provenance at indicator and source level.
- Structured feedback from pilot participants.

Pilot scope excludes:
- Custom model retraining.
- Use of non-public or confidential data unless separately governed.
- Regulatory decisions made solely on BWA output.

## 3) Methodological commitments

BWA pilot commits to:
- **Determinism:** identical input data yields identical output.
- **Rule transparency:** scoring and mapping logic are explicit and auditable.
- **Separation of evidence and interpretation:** outputs are computed first; interpretation is documented separately.
- **Reproducibility:** results can be re-run using the same data snapshot and rules version.
- **Comparability:** outputs are standardized to enable cross-bank and period-over-period review.

## 4) Data provenance principles

BWA uses official or public data sources and enforces provenance discipline:
- Every analytic element is linked to a source category (macro, sectoral, bank-level, public disclosures).
- Data freshness and source lineage are visible through provenance badges.
- Transformations are deterministic and versioned.
- Missing data and proxy usage are explicitly labeled.
- No hidden enrichment with opaque model-generated signals.

## 5) Epistemic limitations

Pilot participants acknowledge the following limits:
- BWA measures alignment patterns under predefined rules; it does not infer intent or causality.
- Spiral Dynamics mapping is a structured interpretive framework and should be used with domain judgment.
- Quality of outputs depends on quality, timeliness, and completeness of official public data.
- Scores indicate analytical position, not moral verdict or supervisory determination.
- Final decisions remain the responsibility of bank leadership, risk functions, and regulators.

## 6) Governance note

Any methodological change during the pilot (weights, mappings, thresholds, or source policy) must be versioned, documented, and communicated before comparative use.
