# BWA Pilot FAQ

## 1) Is BWA an AI model that predicts bank outcomes?
No. BWA is a deterministic, rules-based analytical model. It does not perform AI prediction or black-box learning.

## 2) What data does BWA use?
BWA uses official public macro, sectoral, and bank-level disclosures within a defined source policy and versioned transformation rules.

## 3) Can two users get different results for the same bank?
Not if they use the same data snapshot and rules version. Deterministic logic ensures reproducibility.

## 4) What does Welfare Score (0–100) represent?
It is a normalized alignment indicator of how observed bank profile maps to welfare-oriented stage balance criteria in the BWA rule framework.

## 5) What is Mismatch Score?
Mismatch Score quantifies structural distance between observed stage profile and reference alignment profile. Higher mismatch indicates higher alignment risk.

## 6) How should we read Spiral Gap Analysis?
Read it as stage-by-stage difference between current and reference distributions. Large positive/negative gaps indicate priority zones for management attention.

## 7) Does a low score mean regulatory non-compliance?
No. BWA scores are analytical indicators, not legal or supervisory determinations.

## 8) How reliable are conclusions if some data is missing?
Use provenance badges. When data is partial or proxy-based, conclusions should be treated as directional and validated with additional evidence.

## 9) Can BWA replace internal risk frameworks?
No. BWA complements existing risk, strategy, and governance processes.

## 10) How often should analysis be updated?
Typically per reporting cycle (e.g., quarterly), or when meaningful new public data is released.

## 11) Is methodology visible to pilot participants?
Yes. Pilot materials include methodological commitments, boundaries, and interpretation guidance.

## 12) What is the Sponsor Lab upgrade path?
Sponsor Lab is an optional post-pilot phase for:
- context-specific calibration,
- governance workflow integration,
- expanded reporting and scenario views,
- training and institutional rollout support.
