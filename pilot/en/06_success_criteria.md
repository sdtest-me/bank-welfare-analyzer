# BWA Pilot Success Criteria

## 1) Purpose
Define measurable indicators for evaluating whether the BWA pilot is ready for broader institutional adoption and Sponsor Lab transition.

## 2) Clarity metrics
Target thresholds across participating banks:
- Average clarity rating ≥ 4.0/5 for Welfare Score interpretation.
- Average clarity rating ≥ 4.0/5 for Mismatch Score and risk level.
- Average clarity rating ≥ 3.8/5 for Spiral Gap Analysis.
- Average clarity rating ≥ 4.0/5 for provenance badges.

## 3) Trust metrics
- Average trust in deterministic methodology ≥ 4.0/5.
- Average trust in data traceability ≥ 4.0/5.
- At least 80% of participants confirm methodological boundaries are explicit.
- At least 80% of participants confirm outputs are reproducible in pilot conditions.

## 4) Usefulness metrics
- At least 70% of respondents rate executive usefulness at 4 or 5.
- At least 70% rate risk prioritization usefulness at 4 or 5.
- At least 60% rate regulatory communication usefulness at 4 or 5.

## 5) Operational quality metrics
- 100% of reviewed outputs display provenance badges.
- 100% of pilot reports include snapshot date and rules version.
- 0 unresolved critical interpretation issues at pilot close.

## 6) Sponsor Lab conversion indicators
- ≥ 40% of pilot banks request Sponsor Lab proposal.
- ≥ 30% express intent to integrate BWA into periodic management review.
- ≥ 20% express intent to include BWA in risk committee workflow.

## 7) Decision rule
Pilot is considered successful when:
1. Clarity and trust thresholds are met,
2. Usefulness thresholds are met,
3. No unresolved critical methodology objections remain,
4. Sponsor Lab interest is demonstrated by multiple institutions.
