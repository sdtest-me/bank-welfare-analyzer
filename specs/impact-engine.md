# Impact Engine Specification v1

## Purpose

The Impact Engine transforms raw financial indicators into a systemic impact interpretation.

It estimates:
- long-term societal effect,
- fragility amplification,
- structural extraction pressure,
- sustainability alignment.

The engine is designed for:
- policy analysis,
- bank comparison,
- risk interpretation,
- Sponsor Lab simulation.

---

# Philosophy

Traditional banking analysis optimizes for:
- shareholder return,
- asset growth,
- profitability.

The Impact Engine evaluates:
- public resilience,
- debt dependency,
- adaptive capacity,
- systemic sustainability.

---

| Input | Source |
|---|---|
| Welfare Score | scoring engine |
| Mismatch Pressure | mismatch engine |
| Spiral stages | spiral engine |
| Poverty indicators | macro data |
| Lending distribution | portfolio data |

---

# Impact Categories

## Stabilizing Impact

Characteristics:
- business lending dominance,
- low extraction pressure,
- moderate profit asymmetry,
- resilience support.

---

## Transitional Impact

Characteristics:
- mixed signals,
- moderate extraction,
- unstable calibration,
- inconsistent incentives.

---

## Structural Risk Impact

Characteristics:
- fragility monetization,
- aggressive consumer lending,
- high mismatch pressure,
- extraction-dependent growth.

---

# Impact Tier Logic

| Tier | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 70–100 | Stabilizing |
| 50–69 | Transitional |
| 0–49 | Structural Risk |

---

# Escalation Conditions

Structural escalation activates when:

```text
profitGap > 5
AND
creditConsumption > 40
```

Effects:
- narrative escalation,
- recommendation override,
- severe warning mode.

---

# Output

The engine returns:
- impactIndex,
- impactTier,
- structuralRisk,
- impactDrivers,
- escalationFlags.

---

# Constraints

The engine must:
- remain deterministic,
- remain explainable,
- avoid hidden weighting,
- support calibration tuning.
