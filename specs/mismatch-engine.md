# Mismatch Engine Specification v1

## Purpose

The Mismatch Engine detects inconsistencies between:

- declared institutional positioning,
- observable lending behavior,
- systemic impact patterns.

It estimates whether the bank narrative aligns with operational reality.

---

# Core Principle

A bank may communicate:
- ESG,
- inclusion,
- sustainability,
- innovation,
- national development,

while operational metrics indicate:
- extraction,
- debt amplification,
- fragility monetization,
- short-term optimization.

The mismatch engine quantifies this divergence.

---

# Inputs

| Input | Source |
|---|---|
| Welfare Score | scoring engine |
| Impact Index | scoring engine |
| Spiral stages | spiral engine |
| Lending structure | bank metrics |
| Dividend policy | bank metrics |
| Narrative severity | narrative engine |

---

# Mismatch Drivers

## Extraction Mismatch

Triggered when:
- high profit growth,
- weak public welfare growth,
- high consumer lending.

---

## Narrative Mismatch

Triggered when:
- public positioning implies sustainability,
- metrics imply structural extraction.

---

## Structural Mismatch

Triggered when:
- system stability depends on population over-discipline,
- debt burden substitutes development.

---

# Mismatch Pressure

## Purpose

Single aggregated pressure metric.

Used by:
- narrative engine,
- recommendation engine,
- impact escalation.

---

# Severity Tiers

| Tier | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Low | Alignment mostly consistent |
| Moderate | Strategic tension emerging |
| High | Structural inconsistency |
| Critical | Extraction dominates narrative |

---

# Output

The engine returns:
- mismatchScore,
- mismatchPressure,
- mismatchDrivers,
- severityTier.

---

# Design Constraints

The engine must:
- remain deterministic,
- never hallucinate,
- explain drivers explicitly,
- avoid generic conclusions.
