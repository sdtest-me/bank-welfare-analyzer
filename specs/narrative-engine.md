# Narrative Engine Specification v1

## Purpose

The Narrative Engine converts analytical outputs into human-readable strategic interpretation.

Its role is not cosmetic.

It is part of the analytical system.

The narrative layer determines whether users:
- understand risk,
- trust the model,
- recognize structural patterns,
- perceive urgency.

---

# Inputs

| Input | Source |
|---|---|
| Welfare Score | scoring engine |
| Impact Index | impact engine |
| Mismatch Pressure | mismatch engine |
| Structural Risk Flags | impact engine |
| Spiral Stages | spiral engine |

---

# Narrative Goals

The engine must:
- explain system behavior,
- expose contradictions,
- avoid generic wording,
- escalate severity appropriately,
- maintain strategic coherence.

---

# Narrative Structure

## Layer 1 — Situation

Explains:
- current bank posture,
- societal interaction,
- dominant operating logic.

---

## Layer 2 — Tension

Explains:
- structural contradictions,
- extraction pressure,
- instability drivers.

---

## Layer 3 — Consequence

Explains:
- likely systemic outcomes,
- institutional risks,
- public resilience implications.

---

## Layer 4 — Recommendation

Explains:
- corrective direction,
- strategic intervention,
- monitoring priorities.

---

# Narrative Severity

| Severity | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Low | Stable alignment |
| Moderate | Emerging tension |
| High | Structural instability |
| Critical | Extraction-dependent system |

---

# Narrative Constraints

The engine must:
- avoid generic outputs,
- remain deterministic,
- remain explainable,
- map directly to analytical drivers,
- preserve consistency with impact tier.

---

# Conversion Layer

The narrative engine also supports:
- Sponsor Lab conversion messaging,
- CTA escalation,
- internal-data recalibration framing.

This layer must:
- preserve analytical credibility,
- avoid marketing exaggeration,
- maintain continuity with analytical outputs.
