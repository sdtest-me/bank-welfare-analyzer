# Product Specification

## Product Name

Bank Welfare Analyzer

---

# Product Purpose

Bank Welfare Analyzer evaluates whether a financial institution contributes to:
- sustainable economic development,
- productive capital allocation,
- population welfare,

or primarily extracts value through:
- debt expansion,
- behavioral mismatch,
- structural asymmetry.

The system combines:
- welfare scoring,
- Spiral Dynamics analysis,
- mismatch detection,
- impact modeling,
- narrative interpretation.

---

# Core Product Thesis

Traditional banking analysis focuses on:
- shareholder value,
- profitability,
- balance-sheet metrics.

This product evaluates:
- societal impact,
- behavioral alignment,
- systemic consequences,
- institutional extraction dynamics.

---

# Primary User Problem

Users struggle to:
- understand hidden structural risk,
- interpret behavioral contradictions,
- compare institutional impact,
- identify extraction patterns quickly.

Traditional dashboards are:
- too technical,
- too financial,
- too fragmented.

---

# Product Goals

## Primary Goals

1. Explain institutional behavior quickly
2. Detect structural mismatch
3. Generate decision-grade narratives
4. Create strong analytical differentiation
5. Support conversion into Sponsor Lab

---

# Core Components

## 1. Scoring Engine

Calculates:
- Welfare Score,
- Impact Index,
- structural weighting.

---

## 2. Mismatch Engine

Detects contradictions between:
- stated values,
- actual institutional behavior.

---

## 3. Impact Engine

Measures:
- extraction pressure,
- systemic instability,
- structural risk escalation.

---

## 4. Narrative Engine

Transforms outputs into:
- readable interpretation,
- warnings,
- recommendations,
- consequence framing.

---

# Target Users

## Primary Audiences

- analysts,
- regulators,
- journalists,
- ESG researchers,
- policy organizations,
- banking consultants,
- institutional strategy teams.

---

# Product Positioning

The product is positioned as:
- behavioral financial analysis,
- systemic-risk interpretation,
- institutional alignment diagnostics.

Not:
- a credit-scoring system,
- investment advice,
- a standard ESG dashboard.

---

# Success Criteria

The product succeeds if:
- users understand results in under 60 seconds,
- risk feels tangible,
- outputs differ meaningfully between banks,
- narratives feel decision-grade,
- users request deeper analysis.

---
